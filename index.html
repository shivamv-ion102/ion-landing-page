Not just matching. Not just adjudication.
The whole job.

Most compliance tools find a match and stop, leaving the harder work to your team. Comperian carries the same name through to a sealed verdict, automatically.

01 · SCREEN ✓

Screen against every list.

Every name moves through global sanctions, watchlists, and export-control lists across every major jurisdiction, with the lists kept current automatically. Names, aliases, vessels, and entities are all covered.

02 · MATCH ✓

Match across languages & dialects.

The name doesn't have to match exactly. Comperian works across transliterations, phonetic variants, abbreviations, and nicknames, catching the matches everyone else misses.

03 · ADJUDICATE ✓

Decide if it's actually them.

Once a possible match is found, Comperian compares dates of birth, geography, role, employment history, and network signals to decide whether it's really the same person. Reasoned across every signal, like your best analyst, in under a second.

Things people ask before they say yes.

Is Comperian a replacement for our existing compliance software?
+

It can be. Many teams stop renewing their RPS or screening platform once Comperian is in place. But you don't have to rip anything out. Comperian runs alongside whatever you have today, or replaces it entirely. Your call.

How is this different from the matching engine we already pay for?
+

Existing tools find phonetic or partial-string matches and create a case. A human still has to read the watchlist, compare biographic data, and make a call. Comperian does the screening and the adjudication, and gives you a written rationale a regulator can read.

What if we don't have an API team. Can we still use it?
+

Yes. Drag a spreadsheet into our dashboard and get adjudicated results back. No engineers, no IT tickets. Many teams start there and move to the API later, but plenty stay on uploads forever.

How do regulators feel about an AI making compliance decisions?
+

Better than they feel about an analyst typing free-text notes at 4pm on a Friday. Every Comperian verdict ships with the full reasoning trail, the signals that mattered, the lists referenced, and a timestamp. Reproducible, defensible, exportable. Your humans stay in the loop on escalations.

What about identity verification and KYC?
+

Comperian works with whatever identity data you already have. If your team verifies documents and liveness in another tool, the verified data flows in and we adjudicate against it. If you don't have one yet, Comperian still works on the name and context you give us. Native KYC and KYB are also coming on the roadmap.

What does pricing look like?
+

Per-decision usage pricing. No platform fees, no per-seat licenses, no implementation costs. You pay for adjudications, not for software. Talk to us with a sense of your volume and we'll come back with a number that fits.
